Teacher disappointed in student after just one class period

Today was the first day at school here at Canyon, and while 6th graders were wondering just where the costumed mascots were (many of them crying for no readily apparent reason), one student was truly letting his teacher down. "He entered the classroom with such clear eagerness to learn the ground rules, and passion for get-to-know-you games," said the teacher. "But he seemed burnt-out by the time I asked him to state his name, pronouns, and favorite food, and try as I might to turn him around, he had dug himself too deep a hole to get out of before I told everyone when the signed syllabus was due." At press time, the teacher was reflecting on how sad it was that this student would likely never make up this one-period delay in their education.
